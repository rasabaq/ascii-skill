Van Gogh in glyphs

Four Van Gogh works, four effects from the ascii-art skill. In each film a white line sweeps across: left of it is the painting, right of it is the same painting after the skill has drawn it. It starts on the whole painting, slides until the whole effect shows, and comes back.

Any effect works on any painting, so you can mix them: for example, the dust effect on Almond Blossom.

Paintings: Vincent van Gogh, public domain. Images from Wikimedia Commons (Google Art Project). Cropped to fit and half redrawn by the ascii-art skill.
